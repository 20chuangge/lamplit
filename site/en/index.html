01

It has its own computer

It runs commands, drives the browser and desktop apps, and reads and writes files. You can watch the screen any time; for a QR code or a captcha it stops and hands you the controls.

02

It asks before acting

Each machine has its own approval level. It only touches the folders you open to it, and passwords and app tokens stay in a local vault that even it cannot read.

03

Your own model

DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi, Zhipu, Doubao, OpenAI, Claude and more, with your own key. Big coding jobs go to the coding assistant you already subscribe to.

Hand it the work on your computer

Not just chat. It breaks a job into steps, works through them, and tells you where the results are.

Long jobs, carried through
A job of dozens of steps becomes a checklist it ticks off as it goes; no need to keep typing "continue". Say "stop" and it really stops.
Spare computers, added in one go
An old laptop, a desktop at home, a cloud server: enter the address and an account and it sets itself up. Ubuntu, Debian, Rocky and Windows all work; cloud servers go over encrypted Tailscale or your own relay, and every machine updates with this one. Step-by-step guide →
Watch, and take over
See each machine's screen live, one display at a time. When you take over, mouse and keyboard are yours; click once and it carries on.
The apps you use
Mail (Gmail, Outlook, QQ, 163…), GitHub, Notion, DingTalk, Feishu, AMap, Home Assistant and more, picked by category and connected by following the steps on each card. Every step goes through the app's own interface and can be approved.
Coding on your subscription
Writing programs or changing a project goes to the Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or Qoder already signed in on this computer, on your own plan, with the model you pick.
Pictures, drawings, video
Send a screenshot and it understands it; posters, illustrations and short videos appear right in the chat, from Wanxiang, Doubao, Zhipu, MiniMax, OpenAI and others.
On your phone, even a call
Scan a code with the Android app; away from home it reaches back over Tailscale or a relay on your own cloud server (three ways). Tap the phone button to talk to it like a call. Turn on "Let bots use this phone" and it works the apps on your phone: sending or publishing only once you say so, paying always asks. No app? Message it on Feishu.
Memory, skills, schedules
It remembers your habits and keeps workflows as skills; daily briefings and regular checks run on time. Files, PDFs and sheets it makes open right in the chat.
Adding an old computer

Approval levels

Careful, standard or hands-off, set per machine; dangerous commands are always stopped.

Folders it may touch

Only the workspace and the places you open. Anywhere else it asks, or is simply not allowed.

Passwords stay out of the chat

Sign-ins use a secure form; they are encrypted in that machine's vault and never sent to the AI model.

Every step is logged

What it did, who approved it and when the screen was taken over are all in the activity log.

Three steps to start

Up and running in minutes; adding a remote machine takes about half a minute of typing.

  1. Download and install

    Get the Windows installer, or the portable version that runs as is. The installer is not code-signed yet: when Windows warns you, choose "More info → Run anyway".

  2. Add a model key

    Open Settings → Model, pick a provider and enter your own key. DeepSeek is cheap and good; add a SiliconFlow key too and voice, image understanding and drawing all work.

  3. Say what you need

    For example "file the screenshots on my desktop by month". To put a spare computer to work: Settings → Machines → Add machine.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

Lamplit itself is free and open source. You pay only for the model service you connect (by usage; everyday use of DeepSeek is a few dollars a month or less) and any optional image or video services.

Can I use my Claude or ChatGPT subscription?

For coding, yes: the work goes to the Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or Qoder signed in on this computer, on your own plan. Everyday chat uses the model key you enter. Subscriptions do not allow third-party apps to use them as an API, and Lamplit never reads any subscription's sign-in data.

Where does my data go?

Lamplit runs on your own computers; there is no server of ours. Conversations go to the model provider you choose; passwords and tokens stay in the encrypted local vault and are never sent to the model.

Will it mess with my computer?

Deleting files, running commands and sending messages ask you first by default; it only touches the workspace and the folders you open; formatting a disk or deleting system files is always stopped. All of this is in Settings → Approval and Settings → Boundaries.

Is the app in English?

Yes. In Settings → Appearance pick English, Chinese or "follow the system"; the assistants then reply in that language too.

Does it run on a Mac?

The desktop app is Windows only for now; Linux machines can join as remote machines that do the work. On phones there is an Android app, and an iPhone can open the computer's address in a browser.
